Questions

Can I see it before I buy?
Yes. The live preview is at comfortable-league-488903.framer.app (Home, Stays, a page per cottage from the CMS, Guest guide, Book direct, About us, Booking terms).
Can each cottage have its own minimum stay?
Yes. Each cottage has a Minimum stay field in the CMS (for example 2 nights, or 7 nights in summer). It shows on that cottage's page and in its booking card. Bookings are requests, so you confirm each one by email.
Do I need a paid Framer plan?
No. A free Framer account lets you edit and publish on a framer.app address. A custom domain needs a paid Framer plan.
Are the text and pictures mine to keep?
All text and pictures are sample content for you to replace with your own. The preview photos are AI-generated. Booking requests are emailed to you once you set the form's Send To address (the start guide shows where); payment is up to you.
